Study in Turkey

Why Study in Turkey?

Globally recognized degrees, affordable living, and a country at the crossroads of Europe and Asia.

Globally Valid Degrees

Turkey is part of the Bologna Process — diplomas are recognized across Europe and worldwide.

Affordable

Live well on 400–650 € per month — tuition and living costs are far lower than in Europe or the USA.

Quality Education

200+ universities, many in world top rankings, with modern campuses and English-taught programs.

Multicultural Life

300,000+ international students from 130+ countries — you will feel at home.

Climate & Lifestyle

Three seas, mild climate, rich cuisine and famous Turkish hospitality.

Career Opportunities

A dynamic economy with international companies — plus part-time work options for students.

Cost of Living (approx.)

Dormitory / shared flat: 150–300 $ per month · Food: 100–150 $ · Transport (student card): 10–15 $ · Total comfortable budget: 400–650 € per month. Istanbul is slightly higher; İzmir, Ankara, Bursa and Antalya are cheaper.
