Diploma Recognition

Diploma Equivalency (Denklik)

Denklik is the official recognition of your foreign school diploma by the Turkish Ministry of Education — a required step for enrolling at universities in Turkey.

Step by Step

How We Handle Your Denklik

01

Document Check

We review your diploma, transcripts and passport to confirm everything is ready.

02

Translation & Notary

Certified Turkish translations and notarization of your documents.

03

Application

We submit your denklik application to the Ministry of Education on your behalf.

04

Certificate

You receive your Denklik Belgesi — ready for university enrollment.

Required Documents

High school diploma (original + copy) · Transcript of all years · Passport · Passport-size photos · Residence information. Requirements can vary by country — contact us and we will prepare your personal checklist for free.
